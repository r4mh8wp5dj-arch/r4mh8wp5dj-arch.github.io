01 Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Emir Kaladağ ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and shares information when you play Block Pit (the "App") on iOS. It also describes the choices available to you and how to exercise your rights. By installing or using the App you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

02 Who we are

The data controller for the App is Emir Kaladağ, an independent developer. A postal address will be added here before the App is released. For any privacy question or request, contact us at privacy@playblockpit.com (see Section 14).

03 Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to enter your name, email address, phone number or any other directly identifying information.

Information stored only on your device. Your best score, run history, progress and settings are saved locally on your device so that the App can restore them between sessions. This information does not leave your device unless it is included in a diagnostic report described below.

Information collected automatically. When you use the App, we and the third-party service providers named in this policy may automatically collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language and region settings, screen size, app version, and general performance data.
  • Usage and gameplay events: sessions started, levels and scores reached, features used, and similar interaction data used to understand how the App is played.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and error reports that help us find and fix problems.
  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) and an app instance identifier created by our analytics service. The App does not use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Network information: your IP address, which is processed transiently by analytics and crash reporting services and may be used to derive an approximate (city or country level) location.

04 How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • run the App and restore your progress and settings;
  • detect, diagnose and fix crashes and bugs;
  • understand how the App is used so that we can improve it;
  • protect the App and its users against fraud, abuse and security incidents; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

05 Advertising

The App does not display advertisements and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If advertising is ever added, this section will be updated before the version that contains it is released.

06 Analytics

We plan to use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google LLC, to collect the aggregated usage and diagnostic information described in Section 3. This information is used to understand overall play patterns and app stability; it is not used to identify you personally. These services are not active yet, and this section will be updated with full details before the App is released.

07 Legal bases for processing (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or equivalent law applies, we process personal data on the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): to provide the App and save your progress.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): to keep the App secure and stable, to fix bugs and to understand aggregate usage.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): where local law requires consent for a specific processing activity. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): where processing is required by law.

08 Personal data in Türkiye (KVKK)

For users in Türkiye, personal data is processed in accordance with the Law on the Protection of Personal Data No. 6698 ("KVKK"). Processing relies on the conditions in Articles 5 and 6 of the KVKK, in particular that processing is necessary for the performance of the service you request, is required for our legitimate interests provided your fundamental rights are not harmed, or is based on your explicit consent where the law requires it. Data may be transferred abroad to the service providers named in this policy in accordance with Article 9 of the KVKK.

09 Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age, or under the age at which consent for data processing may be given under local law (for example 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10 Sharing information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only:

  • with the service providers named in this policy (analytics and crash reporting), who process it on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies;
  • with Apple, as part of the normal operation of the App Store and iOS;
  • when required by law, regulation, legal process or a governmental request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of us, our users or others; and
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to the transferred data.

11 International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, an adequacy decision, or the safeguards required under Article 9 of the KVKK.

12 Retention

Information stored on your device remains there until you delete the App or clear its data. Information held by our service providers is retained for the periods described in their own policies; analytics data is typically kept in identifiable form for no longer than necessary to serve the purposes above and is then aggregated or deleted.

13 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your personal data deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal; and
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as your local data protection authority in the EEA or the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu) in Türkiye.

Users in Türkiye additionally have the rights listed in Article 11 of the KVKK, including the right to learn whether their personal data is processed, to request information about processing, to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly, to know the third parties to whom data is transferred, to request correction or deletion, to object to a result produced by automated analysis, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

California residents may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete and correct personal information. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Because the App does not hold an account for you, we may need to ask for information (such as details about your device) to locate data connected to your device before responding to a request.

14 Contact and data requests

To exercise any of the rights above or to ask a question about this policy, email privacy@playblockpit.com with the subject line "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires.

Applications under the KVKK can be sent to the email address above. A postal address for written applications will be added before the App is released.

15 Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page before they take effect. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
