01 Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled in connection with Block Pit: the iOS game (the "App"), the Block Pit website (the "Website") and the emails you send us. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Emir Kaladağ, the developer of Block Pit.

The App has not been released yet. This policy describes the current pre-release version and says clearly which things are only planned.

02 Who we are

The data controller is Emir Kaladağ, an independent developer based in İzmir, Türkiye. You can reach us at privacy@playblockpit.com (see Section 16).

03 The App today

The current version of the App does not send any personal data to us or to anyone else. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics service and no crash reporting service, and it does not track you.

Stored on your device. So that you can continue where you left off, the App stores on your device: your best score, your current run (the pit, its score and progress), your Blockies, coins and squad, your day streak, your settings, and simple play statistics such as how long your runs last. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the App.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the App schedules its reminders locally on your device. No notification server is involved.

Information Apple may share with us. Apple shares crash reports and app usage statistics with developers only if you have turned on Share with App Developers in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements). If you join a TestFlight beta, Apple shares with us the name and email address you were invited with, the feedback and screenshots you choose to send, and crash and session information. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

04 This website

The Website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit it, GitHub receives your IP address and the details of your request in order to deliver the pages, and may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

The Website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts, and its fonts are loaded from the Website itself. If you pick a language, your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage on your own device. Links to Instagram, TikTok and Facebook take you to those services, whose own policies apply.

05 Email and support

When you email us at a support, press or privacy address, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments, and we use them only to reply to you and handle your request. Our email is provided by Google Workspace, a service of Google LLC.

06 Closed beta signup

If you sign up for the closed beta on the Website, your email address and the language of the page are sent to Formspree, a form service of Formspree, Inc., which stores them and forwards them to us. We use your email address only to invite you to the TestFlight beta of the App and to write to you about the beta. When we invite you, the invitation itself is sent by Apple's TestFlight (see Section 3). You can ask us to remove your signup at any time (see Section 16).

07 Advertising

The App does not show advertisements and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If advertising is ever added, this policy will be updated before the version that contains it is released.

08 Planned analytics

Before release we plan to add Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google LLC, to understand how the game is played and to find and fix crashes. They are not part of the App today, and no data is collected through them.

Before they are switched on, this policy will be updated to list the exact events and identifiers that are sent, how long they are kept, the legal basis, and how you can turn collection off. Your saved game, such as your current pit and your settings, will stay on your device; only selected measurements, for example the result and length of a run, would be sent.

09 Legal bases

We process personal data only for the activities described above, each on the following basis:

  • Replying to your emails (Section 5): taking the steps you ask for and our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and (f)).
  • Closed beta signups (Section 6): your consent, which you give by signing up and can withdraw at any time (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)).
  • Delivering and securing the Website (Section 4): our legitimate interest in providing a working and secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Meeting legal obligations where they apply (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).

The App itself does not send us personal data today. The legal basis for the planned analytics will be set out before it starts.

10 Sharing and transfers

We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it for advertising. GitHub (website hosting), Google (email) and Formspree (beta signups) process data on our behalf to provide their services. Apple processes data under its own policy as described in Section 3. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

These providers may process data on servers outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States, under their own data processing terms.

11 Retention

  • Data in the App: on your device until you delete the App.
  • Language choice on the Website: in your browser until you clear its site data.
  • Emails: kept for up to 12 months after the conversation ends and then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Beta signups: kept until the beta ends or you ask us to remove them, and deleted no later than 12 months after the App is released.
  • GitHub logs: kept by GitHub as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Planned analytics: retention periods will be stated before any collection starts.

12 Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Article 11 of the KVKK or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to learn whether we process your personal data, to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority such as the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Türkiye or your local authority in the EEA.

How to use them. For emails you sent us, write to the privacy address in Section 16 from the same email address and we will act on that conversation. Data kept by the App exists only on your device: you can delete it at any time by deleting the App, and we cannot see or recover it. For data Apple holds, use Apple's own privacy tools.

We do not sell or share personal information as these terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA.

13 Children

The App is a puzzle game for a general audience and does not collect personal data from anyone through the App, including children. Please do not send us a child's personal data by email; if it happens, we will delete it.

14 Security

The App keeps its data on your device, protected by iOS. The Website is served over HTTPS, and our email is protected by our email provider's security measures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

15 Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the App, the Website or the services we use change, and always before any new kind of data collection begins. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Where a change requires your consent under the law, we will ask for it separately.

16 Contact and data requests

To use any of your rights or to ask a question about this policy, email privacy@playblockpit.com with the subject line "Privacy request". We aim to reply within 30 days. Applications under the KVKK can be sent to the same address.
