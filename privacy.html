01 About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled in connection with the Block Pit iOS game and its website. Emails sent for support or other contact are covered too. Block Pit is developed by the independent developer Emir Kaladağ.

Block Pit has not been released yet. This policy describes the current pre-release version, and anything that is only planned is marked as planned.

02 The developer

Block Pit is made by one person, Emir Kaladağ, based in İzmir, Türkiye; there is no company or team behind it. Emir Kaladağ is the data controller for the processing described in this policy and can be reached at privacy@playblockpit.com (see Section 15).

03 The game

The current version of Block Pit contains no code that sends data over the internet. It includes no third-party software kits, no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting service, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Saved on your device. So that you can continue where you left off, the game saves your best score, your current run, your Blockies, coins and squad, your day streak, your settings and simple play statistics such as how long your runs last. This data stays on your device and the developer cannot see it. Deleting the game removes it from the device. iOS normally includes app data in iCloud and computer backups, so a backup may still hold a copy, which is kept or deleted according to your backup settings.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the game schedules its reminders on your device. No notification server is involved.

Links. The settings screen links to Block Pit's Instagram, TikTok and Facebook pages. Opening one takes you to that service, and its own privacy policy applies.

App Store. Once Block Pit is on the App Store, Apple will share crash reports and usage statistics with the developer only if you have turned on Share with App Developers in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements). This sharing is run by Apple, not by the game's code, under the Apple Privacy Policy.

04 Planned: Firebase

The developer plans to add Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google, before release. They are not part of the current version, and no data is collected through them.

Before they are switched on, this policy will be updated to describe what they collect in the configuration actually used, including automatically collected data such as device information, identifiers and crash logs, along with how long it is kept and the legal basis.

05 The website

The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement; the developer has no access to these logs.

The website has no cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts and no forms, and its fonts are served from the site itself. If you choose a language, the choice is remembered in your browser's local storage on your own device. Links to Instagram, TikTok and Facebook take you to those services, whose own policies apply.

06 Email

If you write to a Block Pit address (support, press or privacy), the developer receives your email address, your message and any attachments, and uses them to reply and to handle your request. The mailboxes run on Google Workspace, an email service the developer uses, so your messages are stored on Google's servers (see Section 9 and the Google Privacy Policy).

07 Former beta signup form

From October 12 to October 13, 2026, the website had a form for joining a closed beta, run by Formspree, Inc. (see the Formspree Privacy Policy). No signups from visitors were received through it. The form has been removed, the closed beta plan has been dropped, and the website no longer collects any form data.

08 Legal bases

  • Replying to emails (Section 6): the developer's legitimate interest in answering messages about Block Pit (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Handling privacy and KVKK requests (Sections 11 and 15): legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)).

The developer does not process personal data of website visitors: the site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, has no forms, and the developer has no access to GitHub's logs (Section 5). Data saved by the game stays on your device and is not processed by the developer. The legal basis for Firebase will be stated before it is switched on.

09 Service providers and transfers abroad

Personal data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising. It may be disclosed when the law requires it. The services involved are in different positions:

  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the website. It collects visitors' IP addresses itself, for its own security purposes and under its own privacy statement; this is not done on the developer's behalf, and the developer has no access to these logs.
  • Google provides the mailboxes through Google Workspace. It stores the emails you send as part of the service the developer uses.
  • Apple runs the App Store and processes data under its own privacy policy; the developer receives only what is described in Section 3.

Transfers abroad. Google's servers, including those that store the mailboxes, may be outside Türkiye, for example in the United States, so emails you send may be stored abroad. Turkish law (KVKK Article 9) sets conditions for transferring personal data abroad. Google offers data processing terms to Workspace customers, including optional terms for transfers from Türkiye; whether those terms are in place for the account Block Pit uses has not yet been confirmed. This section will be updated once it has been checked.

10 Retention

  • Game data: on your device until you delete the game. Copies in iCloud or computer backups follow your backup settings.
  • Language choice on the website: in your browser until you clear its site data.
  • Emails: kept while your request is being handled and deleted by hand no later than 12 months after the conversation ends. This period is the developer's own choice, not a legal requirement; an email is kept longer only if it is needed for a legal obligation or a legal claim. Deleted emails stay in the mailbox's trash for up to 30 days before they are removed.
  • Privacy requests: kept with the related conversation under the same rule.
  • GitHub logs: kept by GitHub under its own privacy statement.

11 Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example Article 11 of the KVKK, the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to access it, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a supervisory authority such as the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Türkiye or your local authority in the EEA.

How requests are handled. Send your request to the address in Section 15. Where needed, you may be asked to confirm your identity, for example by writing from the email address the request is about. Each request is assessed under the law that applies, and you are told the outcome. Applications under the KVKK are concluded as soon as possible depending on their nature, and within 30 days at the latest.

Data saved by the game exists only on your device and in your own backups; the developer cannot see, change or recover it. For data Apple holds, use privacy.apple.com.

Block Pit does not sell or share personal information as these terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA.

12 Children

Block Pit is a puzzle game for a general audience. The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, and the website has no forms. The developer could receive a child's personal data only through an email. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent personal data, write to privacy@playblockpit.com; the request will be reviewed and the data deleted.

13 Security

The game's data stays on your device, protected by iOS. The website is served over HTTPS. The mailboxes are protected by Google Workspace's security measures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

14 Changes to this policy

This policy is updated when the game, the website or the services used change, and before any new kind of data collection starts, such as Firebase. The date at the top shows the latest version.

15 Contact

For questions about this policy or requests about your personal data, email privacy@playblockpit.com. Adding "Privacy request" to the subject line helps the request get handled faster, but it is not required. Applications under the KVKK can be sent to the same address.
