How it works

A 4×4 pit, eight blocks deep. Pieces fall. You decide where they land.

01

Drop

Drag a piece over the pit, tap to rotate it, let it fall.

02

Match

Three or more of one colour touching, in any direction, and they burst.

03

Trigger chains

Whatever was stacked above drops into the gap. New matches fire on their own. Streaks multiply the score.
